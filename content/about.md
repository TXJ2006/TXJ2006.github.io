# Xujiang Tang (唐煦江)

I am a theoretical mathematics researcher and a machine learning theory enthusiast. My work is centered on mathematics. I am an undergraduate student in **Mathematics and Applied Mathematics** at **Yangtze University College of Arts and Sciences** and a visiting student at **Great Bay University**.

My current mathematical work focuses on algebraic topology, finite group theory, equivariant homotopy theory, and formal proof. I also study machine learning theory from a mathematical perspective, especially generalization, out-of-distribution prediction, and Koopman/operator-theoretic methods for long-sequence data.
