# Research

I study problems where mathematical structure can improve learning, prediction, or decision-making. My current interests include bilevel optimization, implicit differentiation, online learning, nonlinear dynamics, Koopman methods, stochastic processes, and rough volatility.

## Bilevel Optimization and Implicit Differentiation

**Independent research · 2025 - present**

- Investigating hypergradient computation beyond the classical invertible-Hessian assumption by modeling lower-level solution sets as differentiable manifolds.
- Developing a curvature-filter framework for implicit differentiation through projection operators on solution manifolds and singular behavior near focal sets.

## Deep Hedging under Rough Volatility

**Independent research · 2025 - present**

- Developed a fractional-kernel neural architecture for variance-optimal hedging under rough-volatility models.
- Combined stochastic analysis, neural approximation, and numerical optimization in an implementable framework.

## Dynamical Systems and Koopman Operator Learning

**Research collaborator, Prof. Yanbing Jia's group · Mar. 2024 - present**

- Investigating long-horizon prediction for nonlinear dynamical systems.
- Proposed a Koopman Operator Linearization Skeleton for nonlinear dynamical modeling.
- Implemented and evaluated the framework on multiple chaotic-system benchmarks against deep-learning baselines.

## Biomedical Data Science

**Research assistant, Northeastern University (Shenyang) · Sep. 2024 - Jan. 2025**

- Developed clustering methods for spatial transcriptomics under the supervision of Dr. Dayu Hu.
- Improved preprocessing and analysis workflows for high-dimensional single-cell data.
- Built data-processing pipelines for downstream biological analysis.

## Stochastic Modeling and Quantitative Finance

**Team lead, student research project · Oct. 2023 - Apr. 2024**

- Founded and led a student team studying rough-volatility models and Bayesian inference for quantitative finance.
- Implemented stochastic simulation and Markov Chain Monte Carlo methods for parameter estimation.
- Investigated computational bottlenecks in stochastic-process simulation and coordinated technical discussions.

## Earlier Research Engineering

At the **Peking University Open-Source LLM Group** (Jun. - Aug. 2024), I contributed to large-language-model deployment and retrieval-augmented generation, including retrieval and knowledge-integration modules.
