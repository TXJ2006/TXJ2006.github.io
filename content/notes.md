This collection documents my preparation for research in **online learning, bandit algorithms, and sequential decision-making** under the supervision of Prof. Tianyuan Jin. The notes move from the probability of adaptive data to the information-theoretic tools used in regret analysis and lower bounds.

The PDFs are compiled from reproducible LaTeX sources maintained in the [research-notes repository](https://github.com/TXJ2006/elegantbook-tech-blog).

## Sequential Decision Foundations

1. **[Why Bandits Matter](/files/notes/why-bandits-matter.pdf)**<br>
   Sequential decisions under uncertainty and the basic bandit abstraction.

2. **[Regret and the Language of Exploration](/files/notes/regret-exploration-language.pdf)**<br>
   Regret, exploration-exploitation tradeoffs, and analytical viewpoints.

3. **[Probability, Filtrations, and Adaptive Data](/files/notes/probability-filtrations-adaptive-data.pdf)**<br>
   Filtrations, stopping, post-selection effects, and adaptive observations.

## Concentration and Statistical Evidence

4. **[Concentration Bounds for Bandit Analysis](/files/notes/concentration-bounds-bandit-analysis.pdf)**<br>
   Fixed-time bounds, confidence radii, and UCB-style reasoning.

5. **[Martingales and Optional Stopping](/files/notes/martingales-optional-stopping.pdf)**<br>
   Martingale tools for adaptive processes and stopping-time arguments.

6. **[KL Divergence as Statistical Evidence](/files/notes/kl-divergence-statistical-evidence.pdf)**<br>
   Relative entropy, likelihood ratios, and evidence accumulation.

## Models and Lower Bounds

7. **[Exponential Families as Bandit Models](/files/notes/exponential-families-bandit-models.pdf)**<br>
   Canonical parameters, sufficient statistics, and information geometry.

8. **[Change of Measure and Bandit Lower Bounds](/files/notes/change-of-measure-bandit-lower-bounds.pdf)**<br>
   Alternative environments, indistinguishability, and information-theoretic lower bounds.
