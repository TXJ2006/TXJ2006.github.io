# Interests

My research interests are intentionally concentrated. My core direction is theoretical mathematics, especially algebraic topology, finite group theory, and formal proof. My machine learning theory work is secondary and focuses on mathematically structured problems such as long-sequence modeling and operator-theoretic methods.

## Algebraic Topology and Equivariant Homotopy Theory

I am interested in fixed point methods, equivariant homotopy theory, geometric fixed point tomography, Bökstedt periodicity, and stable homotopy-theoretic structures. A recurring question is how global homotopical information can be reconstructed from fixed point, local, or subgroup-level data.

## Finite Group Theory and Quillen-Type Problems

I study finite groups through subgroup complexes and p-local structure. Current themes include Quillen's p-subgroup conjecture, centralizer energy, FRUL-WOS spheres, centralizer structures, and topological methods in finite group theory.

## Formal Proof and Lean

I am interested in Lean formalization as mathematical infrastructure. My focus is on Lean, Mathlib, proof engineering, and the formalization of advanced arguments in algebra, topology, and analysis.

## Foundational Machine Learning Theory

My machine learning theory interests are mathematical rather than engineering-oriented. I focus on generalization, learnability, out-of-distribution prediction, and Koopman/operator-theoretic methods for long-sequence data.
